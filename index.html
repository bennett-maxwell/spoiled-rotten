Risk Assessment

32 / 100

Lower-moderate risk. Scored from five FDD-grounded factors (lower = lower risk):

  • Outlet stability 6/20 — ~10% three-year franchised attrition (2 ceased 2023–2024 vs ~20 average outlets); 1 company-owned ceased and 1 reacquired in 2024.
  • Legal cleanliness 2/20 — zero litigation and zero bankruptcy disclosures (Items 3–4).
  • Fee burden 11/20 — 9% royalty + 1.5% technology fee = 10.5% of gross sales, plus minimum annual royalty ($4,500 year 1 rising to $24,750 year 10) and minimum gross-sales standards ($50,000 year 1 to $275,000 year 10).
  • Financial evidence quality 10/20 — Item 19 figures are unaudited and franchisee-reported, with wide dispersion (bottom-third 2024 average $99,461 vs top-third $558,770).
  • Trend momentum 3/20 — outlets grew 15 to 29 (+93%) and system gross sales grew $3.09M to $5.57M (+80%) from 2022 to 2024; 11 new franchised outlets projected.

Item-19-style figure: 2024 average gross sales $279,597 across 19 full-time locations; median $171,605. Confidence: MODERATE — the figures are unaudited, franchisee-reported, and exclude new, part-time, reacquired, and noncompliant outlets. The FDD states: “Some outlets have earned this amount. Your individual results may differ. There is no assurance that you will earn or sell as much.”
